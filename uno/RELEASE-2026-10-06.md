# UNO interaction and pacing update

## Rules and controls

- Lobby readiness is labelled in green/red, in both themes.
- Only the host can **Force start** after confirmation. It skips readiness, not connection checks, the minimum of two players, or the active-round lock.
- A human's second-last play retains their turn while the card animation completes. The original upstream UNO button then appears for that player only. They have three seconds of exclusive call time, then five seconds in which another connected player may catch the missed call. A successful catch adds two cards. A call can still beat the catch; the host serializes commands and rejects stale/duplicate/late actions. Uncaught windows simply expire. No pre-arming or out-of-turn calls. Bots call automatically. Swapping/rotating into a one-card hand does not open this second-last-play window.
- Jump-ins require identical **colour and number** (red 4 onto red 4, never blue 4 onto red 4). Action/wild cards remain excluded. Movement, draw decisions, penalties and UNO windows block jump-ins.
- Initial dealing, individual draws, plays, swaps and rotations have mandatory host-side pacing. Reduced-motion users receive the same ordered states and deadlines without card flight. The **Motion** control in Settings can explicitly turn movement on or off.
- Duplicate snapshots/acknowledgements must rebind DOM hit targets without resetting in-flight Three.js transforms.
- Seat order is fixed by lobby admission, rotated around the local viewer. Direction reverses progression, not seat placement. Numbered player labels sit above their card piles; the current-turn marker and ordered next-player list make direction explicit.

## Joining and appearance

Invite links go directly to a name-and-join form, never accidentally create a new lobby. Names remain editable before joining. Avatars accept a strict six-digit colour picker value (legacy preset names remain valid) and twelve allowlisted patterns. Crosses/diamonds are centered about the 256×256 tile. Stars, hearts, hexagons and solid are new locally authored SVGs.

The UNO button is the unmodified image used by the original ScuffedUNO game's `uno-btn`: `https://scuffeduno.online/img/logo.648f8b18.png`. See `assets/scuffeduno/uno-button-provenance.json`. Attribution and licence uncertainty remain documented; no new rights claim is made.

## Compact toolbar

- The cog opens Settings, containing motion, theme, credits and the leave/end-room action, on desktop and mobile.
- The hamburger opens a scrollable Recent actions drawer. It retains the last 50 public notices observed by this tab, newest first, after their animations finish. Duplicate snapshots are ignored; hands and draw identities are never copied into history. History is memory-only and resets when leaving or entering a room; it is not a server-side archive or a complete reconnect backfill.
- Healthy connection details live inside Settings; reconnecting and failure messages remain visible above the table.
- The puzzles link appears only on the main menu, not in a lobby or round. The passive hand-privacy footer is removed; actionable play/draw hints remain.
- Both drawers support keyboard focus, Escape and explicit close controls. The game continues while a drawer is open.

## Animation follow-up

See `ANIMATIONS.md`: source-size-preserving curved plays, fast overlapping opening deal, centered discard/ring, action/UNO/penalty callouts, gradual tint/hand spacing, size-aware transfers and animated choices/results. The original's camera zoom and opponent-hand reveal are deliberately not copied. Private hands, fixed seats and host-authoritative phase ordering remain.

## Compatibility and deployment

Invite/application protocol is now **3** (the earlier UNO-rules release was 2), preventing clients from mixing incompatible deal/cue schedules. Refresh all browsers and create a new room after updating. The Rust/WASM byte transport is unchanged. The HTML import map pins this release's first-party modules and CSS with `v=20261006c` to avoid mixing cached old modules with new HTML. There is still no backend or database.

Tests: `tests/uno-release.test.mjs` adds authority, timing, penalties, privacy, conservation, force-start and colour validation cases. `tests/uno-release-browser.cjs` uses two real iroh peers and real WebGL, with clearly marked host-owned test deals for rare states. No production fixture endpoints or mocked networking are added. Baseline pure rule tests explicitly expire UNO windows where timing is not under test. `tests/uno-history.test.mjs` covers bounded, public-only history and duplicate suppression. `tests/uno-toolbar.cjs` exercises settings, history, keyboard focus, long text, scrolling, connection warnings and cleanup at four viewport sizes in both themes.

Physical iOS/Android devices, OS suspension and separate-network play are not certified by headless browser tests.

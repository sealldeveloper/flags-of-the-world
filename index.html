Take your puzzles with you

Setting up offline access…

Opening a daily puzzle saves a copy when storage is available. Use “Save this day offline” for unplayed puzzles too. Saves are browser-local, not account-synced; your browser may clear them.

Made by seall.dev · Scattergories original & attribution

Install the games app

In Chrome or Edge, use the browser’s Install app menu. On iPhone or iPad, open this site in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from the new icon. On Mac, Safari offers File → Add to Dock.

Installation support depends on your browser. Download daily puzzles before going offline. Installing does not sync or transfer saves between browsers.
